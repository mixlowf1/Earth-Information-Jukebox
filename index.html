Empirical Evidence

Multiple independent scientific indicators corroborate the accelerating planetary thermal trend:

🌡️ Surface Warming Rate

Global surface temperature has risen by approximately 1.2°C to 1.35°C above the pre-industrial baseline. The past 10 years are the warmest 10 years on instrumental record.

🌊 Ocean Heat Content (OHC)

Over 90% of excess heat trapped by greenhouse gases is absorbed by oceans. The upper 2,000 meters reached record high heat values consecutively over recent years.

🧊 Cryosphere & Polar Ice

Arctic September sea ice extent has declined by over 12% per decade since satellite records began in 1979; the Greenland and Antarctic ice sheets have lost trillions of tons of mass.

📈 Atmospheric CO₂ Concentration

CO₂ concentrations measured at Mauna Loa rose from ~338 ppm in 1980 to over 425 ppm today, levels unprecedented in at least 800,000 years of Antarctic ice core records.
